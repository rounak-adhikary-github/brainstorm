Thailand tourist visa for Indian passports

E-Visa applies fee-wise from ฿2,000 · 60-day visa exemption for tourism is the usual route — always confirm on the official portal before booking.

Passport valid 6+ months Proof of funds ₹20,000 Return ticket

Documents to carry

  • • Passport with 6+ months validity and blank pages
  • • Return / onward ticket confirmation
  • • Hotel booking or proof of address in Thailand
  • • Funds proof — ₹20,000 cash or equivalent cards
  • • 2 passport photos for visa-on-arrival paperwork
  • • Travel insurance covering medical evacuation

Money & practicals

  • • Carry THB ฿10,000 per person as customs-ready cash
  • • Cards work widely; keep some cash for islands
  • • Airport transfer Phuket ⇄ Krabi ≈ ฿3,000 by road
  • • SIM: 8-day tourist pack ≈ ฿299 at the airport
  • • Tipping ฿20–100 is appreciated, never mandatory
  • • Emergency tourist police: 1155
